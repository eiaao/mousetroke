Choose for your needs

Choose Mousetroke if…

You want explicit Windows 11 support, MIT-licensed source, screen corners and per-app settings.

Choose StrokeIt if…

Your StrokeIt configuration already works well, or you depend on its plugins and Professional Lua workflow.

Differences in daily use

StrokeIt still lists 0.9.7 and fixes for Win95 through Win7. That does not establish that it fails on Windows 11, or promise compatibility with current systems. Check your existing setup in your own apps before migrating.

StrokeIt Professional includes Lua. MIT-licensed plugins do not establish an MIT license for the whole product. Mousetroke publishes its project under MIT and offers directional and shape gestures, per-app rules and automatic fullscreen pause. This does not imply configuration or script compatibility.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good StrokeIt alternative for Windows 11?

Consider Mousetroke if you mainly use a mouse and want system-wide gestures, screen corners and inspectable MIT source. For deep macro editing, native multi-finger input or browser-only use, follow the choices on this page.

Is Mousetroke free and open source?

Yes. It uses the MIT license, with no ads, account or telemetry. It checks GitHub for updates once a day by default; turn that off in Preferences if you prefer.

Can the Windows download trigger SmartScreen?

Yes. The exe is not code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may warn on first run. The README says to choose More info → Run anyway. Download from the official release and verify SHA-256.
