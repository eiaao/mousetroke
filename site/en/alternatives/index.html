Frequently asked questions

How do I choose a mouse gesture tool?

Start with mouse, touchpad or touchscreen input, then decide between browser-only and system-wide use. Compare scripting depth, per-app rules, licensing and installation requirements, and test on your hardware and daily apps.

When should I choose Mousetroke?

When you want free MIT-licensed system-wide mouse gestures, screen corners and per-app rules on Windows 10 1809+/11 x64, or want to try the macOS 11+ beta.

Is a system-wide app always better than an extension?

No. An extension may be enough for web pages. Choose a system-wide tool for other apps or screen edges. For touch-first use, pick a tool that explicitly documents the relevant input device.
